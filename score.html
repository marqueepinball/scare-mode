Follow steps 1 to 3 below.

1Camera and screen corners

Using a GoPro or other USB camera? Plug it into this laptop in webcam mode, then pick it above. The choice is remembered on this device.

Drag the four green corners onto the four corners of the display's glowing area. For a dot matrix, put each corner on the centre of the corner dot. A zoom bubble helps while you drag.

2Box the score

The display, straightened. Drag a box around just the score digits, with a little space around them. Leave out "Player 1", ball count and other text.

What the reader sees. Green boxes are digits it is sure about, red ones it is not.

3Teach the digits

When a score is showing and steady, tap Freeze, type the number you see, and tap Learn. Repeat until all ten digits are green. Scores often switch to a smaller font once they get long, so teach a short score and a long one. Attract mode high scores are a quick way to do it.

Higher rejects more frames but almost never misreads. Lower if it rarely reads a score it has been taught.

4Modes and awards (optional)

When a screen you want to call out is on the display (Multiball, Jackpot, a mode start), tap Freeze, type a name, and tap Capture. When that screen shows up again the TV announces it. Works best for screens that hold still for a moment.

Setup backup

The setup is saved on this phone for this room. Download it to reuse on another phone or machine of the same model.
